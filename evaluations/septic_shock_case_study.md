# Prompt

What are the early signs of septic shock in an adult ICU patient, and what immediate actions should healthcare providers take?

---

# AI Response A

Early signs of septic shock include fever, tachycardia, hypotension, altered mental status, decreased urine output, elevated lactate levels, and rapid breathing.

Immediate actions include:

* Assess airway, breathing, and circulation.
* Notify the healthcare team immediately.
* Obtain blood cultures before antibiotics if possible.
* Administer broad-spectrum antibiotics promptly.
* Begin fluid resuscitation according to sepsis protocols.
* Monitor vital signs, urine output, and lactate levels closely.

---

# AI Response B

Septic shock occurs when bacteria enter the bloodstream. The main sign is fever. Patients should be observed for a few hours before treatment is started to confirm the diagnosis.

Immediate actions:

* Give pain medication.
* Encourage oral fluids.
* Wait for laboratory confirmation before administering antibiotics.
