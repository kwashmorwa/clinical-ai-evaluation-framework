# Evaluation Summary Report

## Project

Clinical AI Evaluation Framework

## Models Evaluated

* GPT-4
* Claude
* Gemini
* Llama

## Evaluation Categories

* Clinical Accuracy
* Patient Safety
* Completeness
* Clarity
* Reasoning Quality
* Hallucination Risk

## Sample Results

| Model  | Accuracy | Safety | Completeness | Clarity | Reasoning | Hallucination Risk | Total |
| ------ | -------- | ------ | ------------ | ------- | --------- | ------------------ | ----- |
| GPT-4  | 5        | 5      | 3            | 3       | 2         | 2                  | 20    |
| Claude | 5        | 5      | 3            | 3       | 2         | 2                  | 20    |
| Gemini | 4        | 4      | 3            | 3       | 2         | 2                  | 18    |
| Llama  | 3        | 3      | 2            | 2       | 1         | 1                  | 12    |

## Key Findings

1. Responses with strong clinical reasoning consistently achieved higher scores.
2. Patient safety failures had the greatest impact on final ratings.
3. Hallucinated medical recommendations significantly reduced evaluation scores.
4. Structured prompts improved response quality across all models.

## Conclusion

This benchmark demonstrates the application of healthcare expertise to AI model evaluation. The framework can be expanded to include additional specialties such as emergency medicine, cardiology, and internal medicine.
